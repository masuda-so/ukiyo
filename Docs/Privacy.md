# Ukiyo Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Ukiyo is provided by Ether LLC. This policy explains how the app handles data.

## Data stored by the app

Ukiyo stores the content you create, such as text, dates, and selected images, in
the app’s local SwiftData store on your device. The app does not include an
analytics SDK, advertising SDK, account system, or remote content database.

When Ukiyo offers a photo picker, the system picker gives the app only the item
you select. Ukiyo does not request unrestricted access to your photo library.

## Image Playground

If you have Pro access and explicitly open Image Playground, Ukiyo provides the
selected photo as visual inspiration, together with an ukiyo-e/woodblock-inspired
concept, to Apple's system Image Playground interface. Image Playground creates
a new image; it does not transform the selected photo. Apple controls that system
feature and its availability. Ukiyo does not use a third-party image-generation
API, send the photo to Ether LLC, or operate an image-generation server.

After you review a generated result in the system interface, Ukiyo temporarily
receives that result. The app stores the selected source photo and generated
result separately in its local SwiftData store only when you choose Save AI
Image. Cancelling or discarding the draft does not save the generated result.

## On-device assistant

On supported Apple devices and languages, assistant requests use Apple Foundation
Models on device. Ukiyo does not send prompts or generated responses to a remote
AI provider or to Ether LLC. Model availability and system-level diagnostics are
controlled by the operating system and Apple’s applicable terms.

## Purchases

Apple processes purchases. Ukiyo requests localized product information and reads
cryptographically verified StoreKit transactions to decide whether Pro access is
active. Ether LLC does not receive or store your payment-card details in the app.

## Retention and deletion

Local content, including any explicitly saved source and generated images,
remains on the device until you delete it in Ukiyo, remove the app, or erase the
device. Deleting an item removes it from the app’s local store.
StoreKit transaction history is retained by Apple under Apple’s policies.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use
[Ukiyo Support](https://ether-llc.com/apps/ukiyo/support/). Do not include private
photos, captions, payment information, or other sensitive data in a support request.

This policy may change when Ukiyo adds a new data practice. The effective date
above will be updated when that happens.
